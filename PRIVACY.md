# iActivity Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

iActivity does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **No network access.** The app makes no network connections. Network statistics come from your Mac's own interface counters.
- **No analytics, tracking, or accounts.**
- **On-device only.** System readings (CPU, GPU, memory, disk, battery, network, temperatures) are read from macOS, shown on screen, and kept in memory only while the app runs. Your settings are saved locally in the app's preferences.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/1mrajeevranjan/iActivity/issues
